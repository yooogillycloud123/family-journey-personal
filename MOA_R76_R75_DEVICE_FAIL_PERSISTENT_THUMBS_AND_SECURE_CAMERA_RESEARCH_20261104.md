## 3. Samsung lockscreen secure-camera overlay: source-proven facts and limitations
- User previously supplied Samsung Camera **17.0.00.43** and Camera Assistant **4.5.00.20** and had earlier APK review for unlocked capture. These exact raw Camera APK bytes are not presently mounted in this conversation; historical review of normal unlocked Camera cannot prove Samsung One UI 9's **secure-lockscreen compositing** behavior. Do not ask user to re-upload merely because a past link is inaccessible; first search original Project/Library evidence.
- AOSP SystemUI `packages/SystemUI/docs/camera.md` explains power button/lockscreen gesture launches **different SECURE_CAMERA intent**, an explicit camera task allowed by the OS. **A system-started trusted secure-camera task is not equivalent to an unrelated app's `TYPE_APPLICATION_OVERLAY`**, even though the phone can open Samsung Camera while locked.
- Android `WindowManager.LayoutParams.TYPE_APPLICATION_OVERLAY` is below critical system windows; the system may change its position/visibility. Android 12+ also supports `HIDE_OVERLAY_WINDOWS` for sensitive apps. These facts establish an **OS-supported pathway for overlay suppression**, but DO NOT prove Samsung Camera 17.0.00.43 actually sets that opt-out on this S26 Ultra build. The user's video alone cannot distinguish keyguard z-order, overlay suppression policy and user lock notification settings.
- R75 source already uses `KeyguardManager.isKeyguardLocked()`, posts a **HIGH importance `CH_EVENT=moa_moment_capture_alerts_v2` notification**, and on user tap `MoaMomentMemoActivity.setShowWhenLocked(true)` attempts to show the memo. It still cannot ensure the **floating pill auto-appears over Samsung secure camera**. R76 APK purposely does NOT try background auto-launch of a new Activity to force above the secure camera: Android background-launch restrictions and the resulting camera interruption are documented, and such a change would endanger user's otherwise successful camera workflow.
- The R76 branch contains **source-only** experimental `CH_EVENT_LOCK` user-configurable high-priority lock channel and privacy-neutral `lastCaptureDeviceLocked/lastCaptureLockChannelImportance/lastCaptureNotificationAllowed` checks. This Java code has NOT passed D8 nor been installed and is NOT a completed R76 feature. Do not claim lockscreen prompt resolved.
- Supported immediate user-facing fallback is R75 already-existing action notification, with Galaxy lockscreen notifications and specific MOA `모아 촬영 즉시 기록` category permitted. Whether notifications are shown **over a running secure camera** is OEM-specific and needs a real device test. No abuse of alarm/call full-screen-intent, Accessibility secure window takeover, root/Shizuku or hidden privileged Samsung APIs.
- To establish whether a normal-app popup can be made to appear in THIS exact secure camera, capture **read-only real-device WindowManager/keyguard/NotificationManager** state while secure camera is foreground and the MOA service emits a capture. Without that, claim "100% possible" OR "100% impossible" is unjustified.
- The technically viable controlled alternative, if exact in-camera guaranteed button is essential, is an **optional MOA-owned camera UI** launched directly via a user action/registered secure-camera activity; its button is inside the same Activity rather than a forbidden third-party overlay. This requires a separate CameraX camera implementation and may not retain all Samsung Camera quality/modes; it has NOT been implemented and must not silently replace Samsung Camera on the side key.
